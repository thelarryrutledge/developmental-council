1Executive Summary

The four-phase architecture (Council → Chair → Executor → Reviewer) is conceptually sound, and its most distinctive virtue — preserving disagreement instead of collapsing it — is real and rare. The problems are not architectural; they are honesty, surface area, and onboarding problems:

  • The headline claim of independent advisors is unverifiable and leaky when one model runs everything sequentially. This run mitigated it with six separate subagents — but the skill never says that's required.
  • "Controlled convergence" is never defined, and there is no end-to-end worked example — the two things a newcomer needs most.
  • There is measurable dead/duplicated surface area (SKILL.md duplicated core/workflow.md; orphaned v2 references).
  • There is no lightweight path and no steering — every invocation implies the full, expensive pipeline.

Recommended path: a single documentation-and-cleanup pass (no architectural rewrite) — reversible, high-leverage, and covering every High-severity finding. Two genuine tensions are left for you to decide.
