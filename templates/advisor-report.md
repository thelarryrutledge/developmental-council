# Advisor Report Template

## Advisor

Name and role.

## Focus

The specific lens this advisor is responsible for.

## Key Findings

No more than 5.

## Major Concerns

No more than 5.

## Recommendations

No more than 5. Keep them specific.

## Must-Preserve Elements

What should not be damaged or overcorrected.

## Confidence

High / Medium / Low, with one sentence explaining why.

## Notes for the Chair

Any tension, caveat, or minority concern the Chair should not flatten.
