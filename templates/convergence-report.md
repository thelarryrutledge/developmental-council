# Controlled Convergence Report Template

## Executive Summary

Brief bottom-line assessment.

## Key Findings

The most important discoveries from the Council.

## Prioritized Recommendations

Ranked list. Prefer 3–5. Do not include minor issues unless they materially affect the outcome.

For each recommendation include:

- action
- rationale
- confidence
- expected impact
- source — either **From advisors** (name the lens(es) that actually raised it, e.g. "Reliability, Operations") or **From synthesis** (the Chair's own observation). Do not invent an advisor source for a synthesis insight. See the traceability rule in `core/workflow.md` Phase 2.

## Areas of Agreement

High-confidence signals independently surfaced by multiple advisors.

## Areas of Meaningful Disagreement

Important tensions that should not be collapsed into fake consensus.

## Human Decision Points

Questions requiring the user's values, judgment, or authority.

## Recommended Path Forward

Suggested sequence of next steps.

## Executor Brief

If an Executor will run, summarize exactly what it should create and what constraints it must obey.
