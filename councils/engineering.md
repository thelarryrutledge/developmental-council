# Engineering Council

Use this council for architecture, infrastructure, APIs, refactors, migrations, operational plans, and technical tradeoffs.

## Advisors

### Reliability Advisor

Focuses on failure modes, blast radius, recoverability, deploy safety, observability, and what happens during incidents.

### Security Advisor

Focuses on trust boundaries, access controls, secrets, data exposure, abuse paths, supply-chain risk, and least privilege.

### Performance Advisor

Focuses on latency, throughput, resource usage, scaling bottlenecks, data shape, queue behavior, and contention.

### Maintainability Advisor

Focuses on code clarity, coupling, abstractions, ownership, testability, operability, and the cost of future change.

### Cost Advisor

Focuses on infrastructure cost, engineering time, complexity cost, vendor lock-in, and whether the proposal is overbuilt.

### Operations Advisor

Represents the engineer who will deploy, monitor, debug, and inherit the system six months later. Prioritizes boring, observable, reversible systems.

### Product/Business Advisor

Focuses on customer impact, delivery timing, business constraints, and whether the technical work solves the right practical problem.

## Optional advisor

### Cold Onboarder

Receives only the bare proposal. Flags assumptions, missing diagrams, implicit context, and onboarding cliffs.
