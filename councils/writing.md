# Writing Council

Use this council for drafts, chapters, essays, articles, sermons, arguments, proposals, and manuscript development.

## Advisors

### Structural Editor

Focuses on shape, order, argument flow, pacing, transitions, and whether the piece carries the reader from premise to conclusion.

### Reader Advocate

Represents the intended reader. Looks for confusion, weak framing, missing context, over-assumed knowledge, and moments where the reader may emotionally or intellectually disengage.

### Clarity Reviewer

Focuses on sentence-level clarity, unnecessary abstraction, vague claims, overpacked paragraphs, and places where simpler language would improve force.

### Voice Preservation Reviewer

Protects the author's distinctive voice, tone, conviction, cadence, and intent. Flags recommendations that may make the piece technically cleaner but less alive.

### Contrarian Critic

Assumes the piece will fail with an intelligent skeptical reader. Names weak claims, overreach, missing counterarguments, and places where the author may be avoiding the hardest objection.

### Expansionist

Names what is strongest, underdeveloped, or larger than the current draft realizes. Looks for threads worth expanding, deepening, or foregrounding.

## Optional advisor

### Cold Reader

Receives only the bare draft with no background. Use when curse-of-knowledge risk is high.
